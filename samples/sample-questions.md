# Sample Questions

## Rule 13 - Overtaking

Question: Which vessel keeps out of the way in an overtaking situation?

Correct answer: The overtaking vessel.

Explanation: Any vessel overtaking another shall keep out of the way of the vessel being overtaken until finally past and clear.

---

## Rule 14 - Head-on Situation

Question: Two power-driven vessels are meeting on reciprocal or nearly reciprocal courses with risk of collision. What action should each vessel take?

Correct answer: Each vessel should alter course to starboard.

Explanation: Rule 14 requires both power-driven vessels in a head-on situation to alter course to starboard so that each passes on the port side of the other.

---

## Rule 15 - Crossing Situation

Question: In a crossing situation between two power-driven vessels, which vessel is give-way?

Correct answer: The vessel which has the other vessel on her own starboard side.

Explanation: That vessel must keep out of the way and, if circumstances admit, avoid crossing ahead.

---

## Rule 19 - Restricted Visibility

Question: When vessels are not in sight of one another in or near restricted visibility, which rule applies?

Correct answer: Rule 19.

Explanation: Rule 19 applies to vessels not in sight of one another when navigating in or near an area of restricted visibility.
