# Annex Coverage v1.7

COLREG Trainer v1.7 expands the training database using the COLREG Consolidated 2018 edition.

## Covered Annexes

| Annex | Topic | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Annex I | Positioning and technical details of lights and shapes | Covered |
| Annex II | Additional signals for fishing vessels fishing in close proximity | Covered |
| Annex III | Technical details of sound signal appliances | Covered |
| Annex IV | Distress signals | Covered |

## Annex I Training Areas

- Height above the hull
- Masthead light positioning
- Vertical spacing of lights
- Horizontal positioning of lights
- Shape dimensions
- Ball, cone, cylinder and diamond definitions
- Light colour and intensity awareness
- Manoeuvring light reference

## Annex II Training Areas

- Additional fishing vessel signals
- Trawlers shooting nets
- Trawlers hauling nets
- Net fast on obstruction
- Pair trawling
- Purse seine yellow flashing lights

## Annex III Training Areas

- Whistle technical requirements
- Frequency ranges
- Sound pressure levels
- Bell and gong requirements
- Sound appliance construction
- Audibility range awareness

## Annex IV Training Areas

- MAYDAY
- SOS
- Orange smoke
- Red flares
- EPIRB / radio distress alerts
- Continuous fog signal
- Raising and lowering arms
- Distress signal restrictions

## Notes

Annex training is included for educational and exam-preparation purposes.

The product does not replace official COLREG publications or certified maritime training.
